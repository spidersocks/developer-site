segment-end-cue.wav
===================

Source: Mixkit “Simple countdown” (sound id 922)
File: mixkit-simple-countdown-922.wav (rebuild)
License: Mixkit License (free for commercial & personal use; attribution
not required). https://mixkit.co/license/#sfxFree

Original Mixkit amplitude peaks were ~2s apart (~0.02 / 2.02 / 4.02 /
6.016s). This asset trims each beep pulse and places them at ~1s spacing
(~0.02 / 1.02 / 2.02 / 3.03s) so playback stays at natural rate (1×) —
three countdown tones in the last ~3s of the exercise, then a fourth
switch tone when remainingSec hits 0 (carry across the segment boundary;
do not pause/seek-to-0 until ~3.4s).

Display-locked playback: arm once per segment against remainingSec;
carry through rem=0 / next segment; no every-frame seek thrash;
playbackRate must remain 1.
