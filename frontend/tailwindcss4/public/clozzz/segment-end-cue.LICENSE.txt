segment-end-cue.wav
===================

Original Clozzz soft ASMR segment-end cue generated for this project:

- Nine gentle wood/pebble-style ticks (~1s apart) for a calm bedtime countdown
- Distinct warmer soft-bell final chime as the next-exercise transition

Designed for the last ~10 seconds of each exercise (OBS Browser Source).
